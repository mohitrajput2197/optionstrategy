Strategy Playbook

Five classic shapes — know your payoff before the market opens

Every strategy in the Lab is one of these five shapes. Learn to read the shape and you know the risk before you know the trade. Pick any card to load that strategy in the Strategy Lab.

Long Call

You expect a strong move up. The risk is capped at the premium paid — the upside stays open.

Long Put

You expect a fall, or you want protection. Profit grows below the strike; loss never crosses the premium.

Bull Call Spread

Mildly bullish. Selling the higher call cuts the premium you pay — in exchange, the profit is capped too.

Bear Put Spread

Mildly bearish. A defined-risk way to play a drift down, without paying for a full long put.

Iron Condor

You expect a range-bound expiry. The credit is yours inside the range; outside it, the loss stays limited.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Why are there three separate portals? +

Because each one does a different job. Index options planning, MCX commodity operations and the team's trade workspace — keeping them separate keeps every desk's data and focus clean, with nothing getting mixed up.

How do I get access to a portal? +

The main and MCX portals open with an invitation code, while the Trade Portal needs an account plus an office code. There is no access without a code — this is deliberate, so the workspace stays with the right people only.

Can I place trades directly from here? +

No. These portals are for planning, strategy analysis, records and operations. Actual orders are always placed through your broker's platform.

Does this page give investment advice? +

Not at all. Everything here — including the payoff tool — is for information and learning only. Take any trade only after your own consideration and advice from your advisor.

Is this the official Anay Investments website? +

Yes — this is the main gateway of Anay Investments. From here you can open the Option Strategy Portal, the MCX Portal and the Trade Portal, each with its own member login.

Risk Disclaimer
